# React Bits components

The school display generates QR codes with `qrcode.react` (ISC license), which
bundles Project Nayuki's QR Code Generator (MIT license). Notices ship in
`apps/web/public/licenses/qrcode-react.txt`.

The locally bundled Spectral and Atkinson Hyperlegible fonts are distributed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their copyright notices and license texts ship in
`apps/web/public/licenses/spectral.txt` and `atkinson-hyperlegible.txt`.

`apps/web/src/components/AnimatedContent.tsx` is adapted from:
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/ts-default/Animations/AnimatedContent/AnimatedContent.tsx

`apps/web/src/components/GlideSelect.tsx` and `GlideSelect.css` are adapted from:
https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/tree/main/src/ts-default/Micro/GlideSelect
